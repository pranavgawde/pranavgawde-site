The state layer — don't ignore it
Most states run their own missions on top of the centre: Kerala (KSUM), Telangana (T-Hub/WE Hub), Gujarat (iCreate, SSIP), Karnataka (Elevate, Idea2PoC), Tamil Nadu (StartupTN), Maharashtra, plus newer entrants like the proposed Delhi Startup Policy 2025 (₹200 Cr VC fund). State fit often beats central fit for a tier-2/3 founder — another reason the matchmaking play matters.
Sources: DPIIT / Startup India, SIDBI, NITI Aayog/AIM, MeitY, PIB, 2025–26. Corpus and cover limits change with each Union/State budget.

These sit closer to funds than incubators — match founders here when they're past prototype and want capital + intensity.
How the matchmaking engine should sort. Four axes decide fit: sector (deeptech → SINE/SID/IITM; bio → C-CAMP; women → WE Hub/NSRCEL; hardware → iCreate), stage (idea → campus/AIC; post-PoC → accelerators), geography (state schemes often beat national fit), and equity appetite (campus 2–5% or grants vs accelerators 5–10%). Arch's value is being the only neutral party reading all four at once.
A starting set, not exhaustive. Programs, equity terms, grant sizes and active status change frequently — verify directly before placing any founder.
